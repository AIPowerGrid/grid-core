# FastH3 Qualification Candidates

## Purpose

Offline-only FastH3 Omni graph template and immutable component provenance.
No recipe here is registered, priced, advertised, or approved for execution.
Startup loading must remain nonrecursive; live parsing rejects the H3 schema.

## Contracts

- `README.md` owns candidate input semantics and outstanding rights/native/
  protocol/billing/UI/rollout gates. Keep it explicit that this is not an API.
- `omni-nvfp4-qad-v1.json` is the fixed Core-owned scaffold for the separate
  `services/fasth3_contract.py` offline compiler. Preserve eight-step sampling,
  model/component pins, 24fps and exact17k+5frames.
- Appearance, opening, audio reference and preserved soundtrack have distinct
  slots. Native guide combinations remain unqualified. No arbitrary graph,
  filename, precision or adapter may come from client input.
- FastVideo's pinned model card selects the MiniMax H3 Community License.
  The reviewed license excludes US/EU/UK/South Korea, including hosted use.
  No separate applicable authorization has been supplied. No additional model
  execution/public serving follows before rights are resolved.
- Qualification bounds describe experiments, not safe capacity. Promotion
  requires rights, native quality/capacity, worker/source protocol, approved
  tariff, funded billing/recovery/refund canaries and reviewed rollout.
